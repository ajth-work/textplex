# RU5000 v0.1

Source CSV: `RU5000 (v0.1) - RU5000.csv`

This is a local Russian frequency-seed export for the TextPlex Russian starter pack.

Use it as a discovery and ranking aid when building the Russian lexicon backend:

- treat it as a seed list, not as the final runtime pack
- keep the workbook out of Git
- use the CSV directly as a seed or normalize it further before generating `resources/lexicon/russian/lexicon.csv`
- preserve lemma, surface form, pronunciation, meaning, and frequency ranking where available
- prefer entries that support basic reading fluency and TRKI-style prioritization

The CSV is especially useful when you want a clean 5,000-word backbone before you expand the pack with corpus examples or dictionary overrides.

For the Russian learning program, the first level should be drawn from a reviewed introductory slice of this list:

- prioritize the most frequent, high-utility entries first
- keep the initial level small enough for a beginner on-ramp
- use the review to pick words that unlock basic reading rather than trying to expose the whole seed list at once

The current runtime slice is defined in `resources/lexicon/russian/program.levels.json` and exposes a 60-item level 1 band through the Russian program API. Keep that manifest aligned with this note when the starter slice changes.
