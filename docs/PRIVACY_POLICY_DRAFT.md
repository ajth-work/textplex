# TextPlex Privacy Policy Draft

TextPlex is a reading workspace built to help people import books, study language, and keep reading progress tied to an account or local profile. This draft explains what information TextPlex collects, how it uses that information, and where third-party services may receive it.

## What we collect

TextPlex may collect or store:

- account details you choose to provide, such as email address, display name, and sign-in tokens
- reading history, vocabulary progress, study activity, and app settings tied to your account or local device
- books, scans, notes, and other material you choose to import or upload
- technical information needed to operate the app, such as request timing, error logs, and browser session state

## How we use information

TextPlex uses information to:

- authenticate your account and keep you signed in
- store and restore reading history, learner progress, and settings
- process imported content into readable pages, vocabulary, and study data
- support translation, romanization, OCR, and generated reading features when those tools are enabled
- maintain the app, diagnose problems, and improve reliability

## Third-party service providers

TextPlex may send limited data to third-party services when those features are used:

- Supabase for sign-in, hosted account storage, and authenticated sync
- OpenAI for OCR, translation alignment, and generated reader articles
- Google Cloud Translate for translation and romanization fallback

See [the third-party data-flow note](./THIRD_PARTY_DATA_NOTE.md) for the current provider list and the kinds of data each provider receives.

## Cookies and local storage

TextPlex may use cookies and browser storage to:

- keep you signed in
- remember the current theme and reader preferences
- restore local settings across page loads

## Data retention and control

TextPlex keeps account and reading data as long as it is needed to provide the service, support your account, or meet operational requirements.

You can:

- sign out of your account
- clear browser storage to remove local preferences and local session state
- stop using hosted features if you prefer a local-only workflow
- contact TextPlex support about account data questions or deletion requests

## Children and sensitive material

TextPlex is intended for general reading and language study. Do not upload material you are not authorized to use, and do not use the service for sensitive content you do not want processed by the selected features or providers.

## Changes to this policy

TextPlex may update this policy as the product evolves. Significant changes should be reflected in the sign-up flow and linked documentation.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact TextPlex support before using the service for material you are unsure about.
