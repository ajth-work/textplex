# TextPlex Signup Policy Draft

TextPlex helps people read, study, and remember material they are entitled to use. When you sign up, you agree to follow the rules below.

For privacy and data-handling details, see the [Privacy Policy Draft](./PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md).

## What you may upload

Upload books, articles, scans, notes, and other materials only if you own them, have a license to use them, or are otherwise authorized to do so.

## What you should not upload

Do not upload material you do not have permission to use.
Do not upload content that violates someone else's copyright, privacy, or other rights.

## Fair use and similar rights

Some uses of copyrighted material may be allowed by law, including fair use or other local exceptions. Those rules depend on the facts and are evaluated case by case.

TextPlex cannot decide fair use for you. If you are unsure, get permission or ask a lawyer.

## How TextPlex may handle content

TextPlex may store, process, analyze, and display the material you upload so the app can support reading and study features.

If content is reported or appears to infringe someone else's rights, we may limit access to it, remove it, or request more information.

## Third-party service providers

TextPlex may use third-party services such as Supabase, OpenAI, and Google Cloud Translate to provide sign-in, hosted account storage, OCR, translation, romanization, and generated reading features.

See [the current data-flow note](./THIRD_PARTY_DATA_NOTE.md).

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for the material you upload and for making sure you have the right to use it with TextPlex.

If you upload content on behalf of someone else, you confirm that you have their permission.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact TextPlex support before uploading material you are not sure about.
