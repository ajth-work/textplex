# Messaging Guardrails

These guardrails keep TextPlex marketing useful, respectful, and low-risk.

## Principles

- Segment by declared intent and observed product behavior.
- Do not infer sensitive traits from usage patterns.
- Keep personalization explainable.
- Let the user control or disable personalization.
- Avoid shame-based, fear-based, or pressure-based language.

## Copy rules

- State the benefit clearly and concretely.
- Prefer calm, direct language over hype.
- Avoid pretending to know the user's background.
- Use the user's own goals when describing why a feature is recommended.
- Do not imply that lack of activity is failure.

## Data rules

- Prefer first-party signals.
- Collect only fields that are necessary for product relevance.
- Make profile fields editable.
- Do not add demographic targeting unless there is a clear, consented reason.

## Example safe explanations

- "Recommended because you selected reading confidence as a goal."
- "Suggested because you often use short sessions."
- "Shown because you paused and then returned to the app."

## Example risky explanations

- "Recommended because we think you are a beginner."
- "Suggested because people like you usually need this."
- "We noticed your behavior and adjusted your profile."
